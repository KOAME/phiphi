October 2026: Exploring Better Everyday Interfaces

October 28, 2026 · PhiPhi Product Team

October was a month of exploration and iteration at PhiPhi. Our product team focused on making everyday interfaces easier to understand and more comfortable to use across different devices.

The work included experiments with navigation patterns, information layouts, task organisation, and lightweight interaction components. Each prototype was considered for clarity, consistency, and ease of use.

October in Numbers

During October 2026, PhiPhi tested 47 interface prototypes and completed 83 internal usability checks. Following these reviews, 12 prototypes were selected for further development.

What We Focused On

Across the prototypes, we explored different ways to reduce unnecessary steps, simplify navigation, and make important information easier to find.

We also reviewed how layouts respond to smaller screens, how clearly actions are labelled, and how users move between different tasks. These considerations will continue to guide future iterations of our products.

Looking Ahead

The next phase will focus on refining the selected concepts, improving accessibility, and exploring how small interface changes can make everyday digital tasks easier.

We believe the best tools are the ones that quietly help people get things done.
